01

Cellular and molecular basis of neuronal resilience

Research

Understanding how neurons remain functional.

Our research focuses on the mechanisms that preserve neuronal homeostasis before irreversible degeneration occurs.

We combine Drosophila genetics, molecular biology, imaging, behavioral analysis, and disease models to identify pathways that protect vulnerable neurons during aging and cellular stress.

Neuronal resilience Maintenance of neuronal function under stress
Proteostasis Protein quality control and ubiquitin signaling
Neurodegeneration Mechanisms shared across age-related diseases

Project

Current research directions

We study neuronal maintenance from complementary molecular, cellular, and systems-level perspectives.

01

Protein Dynamics & Proteostasis

Defining how ubiquitin pathways, deubiquitinating enzymes, and protein-quality-control systems support neuronal function.

Learn more →
02

Cellular Basis of Neurodegeneration

Identifying early cellular defects that precede neuronal loss in models of age-related neurological disease.

Learn more →
03

Neuronal Maintenance & Remodeling

Investigating how neuronal morphology, connectivity, and organelle homeostasis are preserved throughout life.

Learn more →
04

Ion & RNA Homeostasis

Exploring how ion balance and RNA regulation contribute to neuronal resilience and selective vulnerability.
