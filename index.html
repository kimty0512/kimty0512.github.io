The primary goal of our research is to reveal the cellular and molecular basis of neurodegenerative diseases.

Notably, many neurodegenerative diseases are associated with clear and specific neuronal abnormalities during early stages, before massive neuronal cell death.

Our approach provides clues to understanding these diseases and contributes to future effective treatments.
