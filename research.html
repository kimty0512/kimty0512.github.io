Research

Understanding how neurons remain functional.

We investigate the cellular and molecular systems that preserve neuronal homeostasis before irreversible degeneration occurs.

Our approach

From molecular pathways to neuronal behavior

We combine Drosophila genetics, molecular biology, imaging, biochemical analysis, and behavioral assays.

Our central goal is to understand why some neurons remain functional under stress while others become selectively vulnerable during aging and neurodegenerative disease.
